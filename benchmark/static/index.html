How the Rashi score is built

One saved chart is scored three times. The group score is the sum of the evidence lines below. Each line is already a product: source priority × layer × anchor × how close the terms match.

These weights are the three tables written in the Rashi engine. They are not learned by the reinforcement-learning chat trainer. That trainer keeps its own files and does not set these numbers. Prokerala is not called again.
